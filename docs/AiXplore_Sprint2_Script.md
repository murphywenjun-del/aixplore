# AiXplore — Sprint 2 Pitch Script
**Total time: ~5 min 15 sec**

---

## Slide 1 — Title (~15 sec)

"Good morning. We're AiXplore — a multilingual community concierge for senior immigrants. We're a team of four, and today we'll show you the problem, why existing solutions fall short, our approach, and the value we create for three distinct stakeholders. Let's begin."

---

## Slide 2 — The Problem (~1 min)

"Family reunification solves separation. It does not create belonging.

When parents and grandparents relocate to Canada to join their adult children, they face three compounding barriers. First, community life is hard to find and navigate — scattered across dozens of city websites and library calendars, all in formal English. Second, they depend on their children to participate — eighty-seven percent of PGP clients relied on their sponsor to settle. Third, even when they attend, one-off visits don't become habits; networks and routines fade.

Forty-seven percent of 2024 PGP admissions landed in Ontario alone. That's a real population, invisible to current services.

Our north-star: meaningful community participations per active user per month. Meaningful means they attended, and they came back."

---

## Slide 3 — Alternatives (~50 sec)

"We evaluated five ways to close this gap. Only one solves all three.

The status quo is fragmented and English-first. A static directory has no translation and no follow-up. A generic AI chatbot stops at suggestions — no verified feed, easy to copy. A community-centre hub can't scale and is institution-centric. Peer-matching alone hits cold-start and trust problems with no discovery layer.

AiXplore combines a verified partner feed, translation, proactive follow-up, and participation tracking. It's the only approach that solves all three end to end."

---

## Slide 4 — Solution (~1 min 30 sec)

"Our solution is a four-step loop: Discover, Understand, Attend, Return.

Discover: what's happening near me? AiXplore pulls activities from cities, libraries, and NGOs into one verified feed. Understand: what is this, should I go? It explains in the user's language — what's spoken, the cost, what to bring. Attend: how do I get there? Directions, transit, optional peer matching, and a reminder twenty-four hours before. Return: when is the next one? After each visit it asks how it went — and lines up the next session or pivots to a better fit.

Let me show you Mei. She's sixty-four, arrived from the Philippines on a Super Visa. She sees a gardening group three blocks from her daughter's home. She learns in Tagalog that it's free and many people come alone. She gets the bus route and a reminder the day before. Afterward, she says it went well — and she's booked for next Wednesday.

AiXplore runs where parents already are — WhatsApp, WeChat, SMS, or a phone call. It's proactive: it follows up before she thinks to ask."

---

## Slide 5 — Value Proposition (~1 min)

"Three stakeholders, three types of value.

For the beneficiary — parents and grandparents fifty-plus — AiXplore delivers functional value: it saves search and guesswork. Emotional value: she can go alone, she's not a burden. Social value: 'I see Maria every Wednesday now. She's my friend.'

For the channel — adult children — it's a one-time setup. They introduce the platform, then step back.

For the lead paying customer — our hypothesis is cities and public libraries — AiXplore reaches a group they currently miss, including Super Visa holders who fall outside settlement services entirely. Libraries are one of the few trusted institutions these seniors already visit."

---

## Slide 6 — Impact & Next Steps (~50 sec)

"When AiXplore works, the impact is measurable. Feed it to a parent, and we measure the percentage trying a new activity within thirty days. Handle the route and the reminder, and we measure attendance without family help. Ask how it went and line up the next session, and we measure the percentage returning three or more times.

This traces directly to SDG 10.2 — reduced inequalities through social inclusion — and SDG 3.4 — mental well-being through connection.

Why defensible? Translation is table stakes. The moat is the verified, partner-sourced feed and the participation data that compounds with every user. No single institution can replicate this.

What we test next: a manual pilot with ten to fifteen seniors from diverse backgrounds, a ChatGPT side-by-side on Mei's question, and early conversations with library and city staff.

Family reunification solves separation. AiXplore turns arrival into belonging — one return visit at a time.

Thank you. We'd love your questions."
